## Decision checkpoint

- **≥3 of 6** confirm missed calls as top-3 pain **with a real past example and a lost job**
  → proceed to the build.
- **<3 of 6** → do not build. Re-segment or change the wedge. Per Ries this is validated
  learning, not failure.

## Anti-patterns

- ⚠️ **Averaging away the signal.** Six lukewarm "yeah I suppose" answers do not add up to one
  strong pain. Count specific past events only.
- ⚠️ **Letting Dad's enthusiasm carry the gate.** He is one data point out of six, and the most
  biased one.
- ⚠️ **Recording what you hoped to hear.** Fill the sheet within an hour, before memory edits it.
- ⚠️ **Treating "sounds good" as commitment** (Fitzpatrick). Only time, reputation or cash count.

## Open questions

- If missed calls are *not* top-3, what *is* the top pain across the six? That is the pivot candidate.
- Do the trades differ enough that a single wedge cannot serve them all?
